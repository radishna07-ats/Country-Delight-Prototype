## 2. How the business works (as observed and published)

1. **Subscription-first dairy.** Customers subscribe to products Daily, Weekly (e.g. "every Thursday"), Alternate Day (FAQ) or One Time, from the product page (EV-015, EV-016). Subscriptions run every morning until paused, put on vacation or removed (EV-023).
2. **Prepaid wallet ("CD Money").** Orders are charged to the wallet **after delivery** ("Amount will only be charged after the products are delivered", EV-017). Top-ups by UPI/cards/netbanking/food cards, or automatically through **Smart Recharge** (Smart Plan or Monthly Once) and **UPI Autopay** with cashback that is often **split across future transactions** (EV-031). A low balance puts deliveries at risk (EV-014). The FAQ says post-paid subscriptions also exist.
3. **VIP membership.** A paid plan (15/30/60/90 days) giving 20–40% member pricing, with a **benefit cap** that can end a plan early, no cancellation, no clubbing with other offers, and limited pauses (EV-010, EV-029, EV-034).
4. **Two fulfilment modes.** *Morning* (next-day, 5:00–7:30 AM) and *Instant* (30–40 minutes where available) (EV-035).
5. **Growth levers.** Referral ("earn up to ₹1,500"; "Members earned ₹19.33 Crore"), Offer Zone with promo codes, Free Zone cart thresholds, 4+4 / 5 pe 5 subscription offers, VIP, health-goal and protein merchandising, trial packs (EV-027, EV-028, EV-036).
6. **Trust assets.** Product test reports from an independent lab, a milk-testing kit, "140+ quality tests" messaging (EV-032).
7. **Support.** Helpline and in-app chat 6 am–9 pm; Ayuh, a menu-driven assistant with ticketing and "Talk to an agent → Call Us"; email with 24–48 h closure (EV-002, EV-033).

## 3. Unit-economics logic (why the wallet and subscriptions matter)

- **Cash flow:** prepaid balances fund operations ahead of delivery (secondary commentary, R-04). Smart Recharge minimums (e.g. ₹3,000 shown) and cashback are levers on this.
- **Predictable demand:** subscriptions let the supply chain plan milk procurement and route density for the morning run.
- **Retention is the business model.** A subscriber who churns takes a daily recurring order with them. Anything that lowers the effort to *stay* (pause instead of remove, vacation instead of cancel, a resolved complaint instead of an uninstall) protects revenue.

These are reasons to expect high value from **retention, wallet clarity and frictionless subscription changes**, the areas where Graine's agents can act. They are not measurements. Country Delight's own cohort data is required to size them (see [`../04_Revenue_and_Financial_Model/Financial_Assumptions.md`](../04_Revenue_and_Financial_Model/Financial_Assumptions.md)).

## 5. Implications for Graine

1. Position Graine as **retention and trust infrastructure** for a subscription business, not a chatbot.
2. Every proposal must respect **what customers already like**: the product, the morning ritual, and self-serve subscription control.
3. The money surface (wallet, VIP, cashback) has **clear published rules that customers do not understand in the moment**. Grounded explanation is the highest-leverage, lowest-risk first job for an agent.
