Purpose: identify **transferable patterns**, not features to copy. Facts about competitors come from press or company sources (R-xx); anything not established is marked.

**Read-across.** Voice and vernacular ordering is becoming expected in Indian grocery during 2026 (R-10..R-13). Country Delight's differentiator is not speed (Instant exists but is secondary); it is the **daily subscription relationship**. The competitive question is therefore *who makes managing that relationship easiest*, not who has a chatbot.

> Global uplift figures (R-15..R-17) are self-reported, correlational, and come from very different businesses. They are **not** used as Country Delight targets in the financial model.

## 2. Transferable patterns

| # | Pattern | Seen at | Why it transfers | Where we use it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | **Goal-based cart building** ("a week of high-protein breakfasts") | Instacart assistant (R-17, R-32) | Country Delight already merchandises by health goal (EV-036) | UC6 |
| T2 | **Voice in the customer's own language**, including Hinglish code-switching | Swiggy × Sarvam (R-10) | Multi-city base; reviews show language-mismatch frustration in support | UC2, UC3 |
| T3 | **Conversational ordering outside the app** (WhatsApp, phone) | JioMart WhatsApp, Swiggy phone ordering | Reaches customers locked out of the app (OTP/login complaints) and older users | UC1, UC2 |
| T4 | **Confirmation cards with undo** for account changes | Common in fintech and agentic commerce | Reviews fear unrequested changes (N10) | UC5 |
| T5 | **Proactive, event-triggered notifications** (not campaigns) | Delivery-tracking norms in quick commerce | "Marked delivered but not received" and "cancelled without intimation" complaints | UC2, additional A1 |
| T6 | **Pause/vacation instead of cancel** save flows | Subscription-economy norm | Cancellation screen shows no save step (EV-024) | Additional A2 |
| T7 | **Benefit-balance transparency** for memberships | Membership programmes | VIP benefit cap surprises (N02) | UC1 |
| T8 | **Assistant that completes tasks, not just answers** | Sparky / Rufus / Instacart | Ayuh routes to tickets and phone; it does not act (EV-033) | UC3 |

## 3. What not to copy

- **Aggressive in-app upsell pop-ups.** Reviews already penalise forced VIP prompts (N02). An agent that pushes offers unprompted would inherit that resentment.
- **Bot-first support with no human path.** Customers explicitly dislike "AI chat bots" that loop (N09).
- **10-minute speed positioning.** It is not Country Delight's core promise (R-07).

## 4. Implication for positioning

Country Delight can be the first subscription-dairy brand where a customer can **say** "pause milk till Sunday, add one paneer tomorrow" and **see** it confirmed on screen. That is a differentiator that quick-commerce assistants, built for one-off carts, are not designed around.
