# Journey A — Existing Country Delight Experience

Observed in the recording (S3) and the public FAQ (S2). One journey per use case. "Taps" are approximate counts from the recording.

## UC1 · "Why was money deducted?"
| Stage | Customer action | System | Friction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Notice | Sees balance drop / Autopay debit SMS | Balance chip on Home | Autopay deducts 25–26 h after trigger (EV-009) |
| Investigate | Menu → Transactions | Ledger "Paid for Order −₹49 · closing ₹112" | No product/order link (EV-026) |
| Seek help | Menu → Ayuh → "issue with my Recharges" | Three fixed reasons | Real reason may not be listed |
| Escalate | "Something else" → ticket, or "Talk to an agent" → Call Us | Dialer opens; 6 am–9 pm | Leaves app; wait |
| Resolve | Explanation or refund to wallet / bank (5–7 days via support) | — | Slow; trust eroded |
