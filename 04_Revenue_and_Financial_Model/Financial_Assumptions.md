# Financial Assumptions & Methodology

Workbook: [`CountryDelight_Revenue_Model.xlsx`](CountryDelight_Revenue_Model.xlsx) (generated by [`build_model.py`](build_model.py); every output is a formula).

## 1. Ground rules

1. **No Country Delight internal metric was supplied.** Every baseline figure is *illustrative* and must be replaced with Country Delight's data (Inputs, section A). The model is a structure for the conversation, not a forecast.
2. **Impact rates are hypotheses** to be measured in the pilot against a control group (Pilot_Recommendation.md). None is a promise.
3. **Graine prices are the published rates** (graine.ai/pricing, 21 Nov 2026): ₹6 per connected minute all-in, ₹10 per chat session ≤5 min, excl. GST. Volume discounts are set to 0% until Graine issues a proposal. No other client's commercial terms are used.
4. **Revenue ≠ profit.** Net benefit and ROI use contribution (revenue × margin) plus avoided support cost, minus running cost and one-time investment.
5. **No double counting** (see §4).

## 4. Calculation method

| Stream | Formula (monthly run-rate) | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Support savings | deliveries × contacts/1,000 × agent share × containment × cost per contact | Operational saving, not revenue |
| A1/UC1 holds prevented | customers × hold rate × reach × prevented × days lost × value per delivery | Revenue protected |
| UC2 retention | customers × churn × service share × agent share × reduction × ARPU × months credited | **Steady state: extra active customers = monthly inflow × months** |
| A2 save flow | min(subscribers × cancel intent × reach × save rate, voluntary churners × reach) × ARPU × months | Acts only on **voluntary** churners, disjoint from UC2 |
| UC5 expansion | in-app users × expansion rate × value × months persisting | Steady state |
| UC3 AOV | in-app users × assisted orders × value per delivery × uplift | — |
| UC4 campaigns | audience × engage × convert × first-month value × **incrementality** | Only the incremental share counts |
| UC6 wellness | profiles × conversion × value × months | — |
| Growth haircut | (UC3 + UC4 + UC5 + UC6) × (1 − haircut) | Same purchase can't count twice |
| Graine usage | (voice minutes × ₹/min + chat sessions × ₹/session) × (1 − discount) | Voice from support, in-app and campaigns; chats from support, in-app, campaign follow-ups and wellness |
| Year 1 | (benefits and variable costs) × 12 × ramp; fixed costs × 12; plus one-time | — |
| ROI | Year-1 net benefit ÷ (one-time + Year-1 running cost) | — |
| Payback | one-time ÷ monthly net benefit at Year-1 realisation | Not at full run-rate |

## 5. What to replace first

The Sensitivity sheet ranks drivers by their effect on Year-1 net benefit (±25%): **contribution margin, ARPU, containment, cost per contact, agent share of contacts, chat-session price**, then UC5 expansion. Country Delight's margin, ARPU and support cost data, plus pilot-measured containment, will move the case more than anything else.
