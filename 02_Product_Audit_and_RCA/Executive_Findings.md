# Executive Findings — Product Audit

**For:** Graine leadership and the Country Delight meeting team · **Basis:** 7-minute recording of app v10.9.100, 2,150 public reviews (633 negative reviews read in full), Country Delight's public FAQ, Graine documentation.

## The five findings

1. **Country Delight's product and morning reliability are loved; preserve them.** Quality/taste, range and on-time delivery dominate positive reviews. The app's subscription flow works. Graine should add a layer, not redesign the app.
2. **Money moves faster than understanding.** Wallet, VIP, cashback and Autopay each follow published rules, but the ledger shows "Paid for Order −₹49" without the why. Wallet/deduction themes appear in 17–28% of negative reviews, often worded as "fraud". **Explaining is the gap, and it is read-only and low-risk to fix.**
3. **The morning has an hour with no one to talk to.** Deliveries run 5:00–7:30 AM; support opens at 6 AM; self-help ends in a ticket or a phone number. Missing or damaged items in that window become repeat contacts and churn risk.
4. **Customers want control, with confidence.** Pause and vacation are praised, yet changes are spread over three surfaces and consequences (offers voided, items auto-added) are not previewed. A confirm-and-undo change card answers both.
5. **Outreach is already a complaint.** Repeated promotional calls appear in reviews. Any AI outreach must be consent-first and frequency-capped, or it will amplify an existing harm.

## Two corrections to our own proposal before the meeting

- The executive brief says customers "rarely complain about quality". **Our data says the opposite** (largest negative theme). Remove the line.
- The brief proposes **4:30 AM voice calls** for stockouts. Customers already complain about 5 AM bells and calls, and early commercial calls carry regulatory risk. Replace with **opt-in WhatsApp/push**.
